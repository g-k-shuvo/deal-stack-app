FAQ

Common questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes — all plans include a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. During early access, trial periods may be extended.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.

What counts as an "active deal"?

Any deal in your pipeline that is not archived or closed. You can archive completed deals at any time to free up slots on the Starter plan.

Is my client data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit with 256-bit AES encryption. We never use your deal data to train our models without explicit consent.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes — pay annually and save 20% on any plan. Annual plans are billed upfront and are non-refundable after 30 days.

What integrations are available?

DealStack integrates with Gmail, Outlook, Dropbox, Google Drive, DocuSign, and major CRMs. The Firm plan includes API access for custom integrations.

Can I use DealStack as a solo advisor?

Absolutely — the Starter and Professional plans are built with solo practitioners in mind. No team required.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You retain access to export all your data for 30 days after cancellation. After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted from our servers.
